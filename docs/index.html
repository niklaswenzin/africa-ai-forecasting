This question resolves **YES** if **either of the first two qualifying IPC Sudan acute food insecurity products** classifies **at least one area in Famine (IPC Phase 5)** at the area level, for any analysis or projection period falling on or after 1 June 2026. It resolves **NO** if the first two qualifying products both make the relevant area-level classifications but neither classifies any area in Famine (IPC Phase 5) — or if only one qualifying product has been published by 31 December 2026 and it classifies no area in Famine. A "qualifying IPC Sudan acute food insecurity product" is an IPC-produced Sudan product (a full IPC analysis, an IPC Global Initiative Special Snapshot, or an IPC Alert), published on the IPC website (ipcinfo.org) **after 14 May 2026**, that (a) makes area-level IPC phase classifications for Sudan and (b) whose current period or a formally classified projection period covers any time on or after 1 June 2026 (the June–September 2026 lean season or later). The "first two qualifying products" are the first two such products in publication order. A YES resolves as soon as either of them classifies an area in Famine. A Famine determination counts if it is made **either** by the IPC analysis team **or** by the Famine Review Committee (FRC) within that product. A **formal projected Famine classification** (an IPC Phase 5 / Famine classification assigned to a projection period) counts. An area flagged only as **"at risk of Famine"** does **not** count — IPC states risk of Famine is not a classification. Household-level IPC Phase 5 (Catastrophe) population figures do **not** count; an area-level Famine classification is required. Any IPC analysis unit — a town, locality, or IDP settlement — classified in Phase 5 Famine satisfies "at least one area".
