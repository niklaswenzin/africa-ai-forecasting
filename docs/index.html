Forecasts come from Claude (claude-sonnet-5) and ChatGPT (gpt-5.6-luna). Both receive the same question, the same resolution criteria and the same web-search tool, with prediction-market sites blocked. The benchmark is never passed into the prompt.

Every benchmark here is a Polymarket price: real money at stake, not a poll. The number next to each question is the volume traded on that market, and it is worth reading first. A price resting on a few hundred dollars moves on a single small trade and says little; those are marked.
